# Review of "Buckets, armour and uptime decide Warframe builds"

Independent review, 2026-10-05. The reviewer did not take part in the research. Sources used for this review: the report, the eight research notes, `HANDOFF.md` and `damage-rules.md` (the three in-game validations treated as ground truth), the bundled `warframe-public-export-plus` 0.6.8 and `@wfcd/items` 1.1276.6 data on disk, and wiki.warframe.com. Wiki pages marked **(raw)** below were read as raw wikitext (`?action=raw`) and quoted verbatim; pages marked **(query)** were read through a quoting fetch tool and carry the same caveat the report attaches to WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY. The faction overview table on the Damage page could not be rendered by that tool either, so the Scaldra, Techrot and Anarch rows remain unverified by this review. Overframe was not used. No game, app or inventory file was touched.

## Verdict

The report is trustworthy on the shape of the damage pipeline and on most of its arithmetic: every headline number that this review recomputed (the armour table and its 10x, the 42-fold spread, the +165% to +36% claim, crit tier and headshot multipliers, the faction double dip, the Galvanized full-stack values, the capacity and Riven examples, the Poisson status table) reproduces, and the primary-source spot checks of the armour formula, the Viral multiplier, the headshot-crit formula, the level-scaling curves, the Galvanized values, the Deadhead page and the Deep Archimedea restrictions all agree with it. It is less trustworthy on the enemy side and on Archimedea, where it inherits a wrong solo multiplier (x2.0 where the wiki's own wording gives x2.5), mixes units from two different rows into one range, overstates how universal the 2,700 armour cap is, and presents the Condition Overload hitscan/projectile rule more confidently than the wiki does. It also upgrades a handful of labels (summariser reads and inferences presented as confirmed) and leaves one conflict open that the wiki's patch history settles. Used as a map of what to model and in what order, it is sound; used as a source of numbers to hard-code, the Archimedea, Condition Overload, Lich-bonus and exclusivity sections need the corrections and in-game checks listed below first. Nothing in it contradicts the three in-game validations, and several of its rules explain them (Primary Merciless's +30% reload speed, confirmed in the Public Export on disk, turns Trumna Prime's computed 4.00 s into the arsenal's 3.1 s).

## Errors

Each item quotes the report line, names the section, and gives the evidence.

**1. The Archimedea solo health multiplier is x2.5, not "x2.0 or x2.5".**
Section "Level scaling, the mode multipliers and the reference targets": "If the 50% is counted per additional member, solo is x2.0; if the solo player counts as a squad member, solo is x2.5"; the table column "Solo if x2.0 (x2.5 is 25% higher)"; the executive summary's "add 2x to 4x health by squad size"; and priority item 1's "x2.0 or x2.5 solo to x4.0 by squad". The Deep Archimedea page **(raw)** says: "Enemy Health and Shields increased by 100% and an additional 50% for each squad member, stacking to a maximum of 300%." The stated maximum is reached at four players only if every member counts: 100% + 4 x 50% = 300%. Under the "per additional member" reading the four-player maximum would be 250%, which contradicts the sentence. The endgame note's five-value list ("x2.0 solo, x2.5, x3.0, x3.5 ... x4.0") is the error the report inherited and half-corrected. The hedge should be dropped: solo x2.5, two x3.0, three x3.5, four x4.0. Every "solo" figure in the Archimedea table is 20% low.

**2. The Elite Archimedea health range mixes two different enemies.**
Same section: "an Elite Archimedea Heavy Gunner has roughly 1.5x to 3.5x the raw health of its Steel Path level-175 counterpart (474k to 1.12M against 321k)". The report's own table two lines above gives the level-400 Grineer Heavy Gunner as 237k base, 474k solo and 948k in a full squad. 1.12M appears nowhere in that table; it is the enemy-defences note's *Corrupted* Heavy Gunner at level 400 with its speculative x2.5 column. With the corrected solo multiplier the Grineer range is 594k to 948k, i.e. 1.8x to 2.9x.

**3. The Galvanized Scope stack-expiry conflict is settled, not "UNKNOWN".**
Section "Conditional mods and the uptime experts assume": "The 2026 behaviour is **[UNKNOWN]** to both researchers; the wiki statement is the more recent", and the contradictions table's "Wiki is newer; verify". The Galvanized Scope page **(raw)** states "Unlike most other galvanized mods, Galvanized Scope stacks each have their own duration", and its patch history records Hotfix 31.6.4: "Fixed stackable buffs for Galvanized Scope/Crosshair being removed all at once when the first stack's time runs out." TheKengineer's 2021-07-24 observation that all stacks expire together was a bug fixed in 2022. The advisor's uptime default for Scope and Crosshairs should be individual stack timers.

**4. "Every armoured enemy at Steel Path level sits at the 2,700 armour cap" is false for light units.**
Executive summary, repeated in priority item 1 ("Armour state alone is worth up to 10x"). The armour curve the report cites (`f2 = 1 + 0.4 x d^0.75`, confirmed on the Enemy Level Scaling page **(query)**) puts a 100-base-armour unit such as a Lancer at about 1,960 armour at level 175, which is DR 0.9 x sqrt(1960/2700) = 76.7%, a 4.3x effective-health multiplier, not 10x. At the ordinary Steel Path star-chart band (level 100 to 130) the same unit has 1,200 to 1,570 armour (DR 60% to 69%). A 100-armour unit only reaches 2,700 at roughly level 268. The report's own Armour section says "a 100-base-armour unit reaches roughly 1,960 to 2,700 at Steel Path levels" and so contradicts its summary. The 10x figure is right for Heavy Gunners, Bombards, Elite Lancers (200 armour caps by level 175) and bosses; the ranking between builds against trash is less armour-dominated than the summary implies.

**5. The Condition Overload default for unlisted projectile attacks is stated backwards.**
Section "Gun Condition Overload is three different mods depending on the attack": "On most projectile attacks it multiplies". The Condition Overload (Mechanic) page **(query)** does say "Projectile-based attacks tend to have CO-bonuses apply to them multiplicatively", but it also says, immediately above its per-weapon table, "Listing only discrepant attacks. Anything not listed should be assumed to be Additive with +100% bonus ('intended' behavior)", and that table lists many projectile weapons as Adding (Acrid, Kunai, Mara Detron, Tenet Detron, Laetum in both modes, Tysis, Cestra, Furis Incarnon, Ballistica charged shots at 25% to 50%). The page is internally inconsistent; the report picked the stronger sentence and presents it as the rule. For a calculator the safe default for an unlisted attack is adding, with multiplying applied only to listed weapons. The difference is not small: with Serration and +160% CO the two forms give 425 and 689, a 62% overstatement if the wrong default is used. The Galvanized Aptitude page **(raw)** uses the same careful wording: "usually additive with other damage mods like Serration, with exceptions where it is multiplicative."

**6. "Thirteen melee arcanes exist" is labelled WIKI-CONFIRMED but only twelve are enumerated.**
Section "Melee is a different calculator". The melee note's takeaway says thirteen; its WIKI-CONFIRMED list names twelve (Influence, Duplicate, Crescendo, Exposure, Animosity, Vortex, Retaliation, Fortification, Afflictions, Doughty, Careen, Assimilation). The report repeats the count without the list.

**7. "DE's export, `@wfcd/items`, warframestat.us and the wiki all hold the same per-rank text" is wrong for the wiki.**
Section "Data: every stat has a machine-readable home except what mods do". The data note (section 3) says of `Module:Mods/data`: "Only the max-rank description is stored; there are no per-rank numbers", and of `Module:Arcane/data`: "Max-rank text only." Only `@wfcd/items` carries per-rank `levelStats`.

**8. The executive summary overstates the inventory link in Archimedea.**
"three offered weapons per slot drawn from the player's own inventory, which makes 'which of these three, with the mods I own, this week' the question an inventory-aware advisor is uniquely placed to answer." The Deep Archimedea page **(raw)**, which the report quotes correctly in its own Elite Deep Archimedea section, says the offered loadout "can consist of equipment that the player doesn't own. The first column of Loadout parameters will always attempt to be a Warframe or weapon the player currently owns." At most one of the three per row is guaranteed owned. The use case survives, but the offered-loadout mode (priority item 7) must handle unowned offers and the "drop the row" trade-off is more often forced than the summary suggests.

**9. The research-points rule is right, but the wiki is stale on it and the report does not say so.**
Section "Elite Deep Archimedea": "1 per parameter per mission across eight parameters ... maximum 37, with the Legendary reward pool at 34". The Deep Archimedea page **(raw)** confirms this through its Hotfix 44.0.3 entry ("Using a Warframe that matches those in the Individual Parameters rewards 1 Research Point once more ... Legendary Reward at 34 Research Points"), but the Archimedea (Mission) page **(raw)** still reads "1 Research Point per equipment per mission (3 Research Points for Warframes)", the pre-revert rule. Anyone re-reading the wiki will find both. The 44.0.3 patch note is the authority; the report should cite it rather than the page text.

## Overstated or weakly supported claims

**Label upgrades (a note's caveat dropped or a weaker label promoted).** Each would mislead someone hard-coding from the label alone.

- "**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Primary and Secondary Merciless ..., Deadhead ... and Dexterity ... all state 'the damage bonus stacks additively with other damage mods like Serration'" (section "What shares Serration's bucket"). This review confirmed the sentence on the Primary Deadhead page **(raw)** and the pipeline note cites the Merciless page. No note quotes the Dexterity page or any Secondary page; the expert note says "Secondary and melee damage arcanes ... were not checked on the wiki." Plausible, not confirmed for Dexterity and the Secondary trio.
- "**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** ... Acolytes take at most 4 stacks of any status (Impact 3)" and "**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Archons are 'immune to armor reduction'" (sections "Putting a number on status chance" and "Armour"). Both the status note (line 9) and the enemy-defences note mark the Acolytes and Archon Hunt pages as read "via summary". By the report's own scheme these are WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY.
- "**[VERIFIED-ON-DISK]** WFCD's `Enemy.json` uses pre-2024 health-type names and is excluded from the app's build; PEP's `factionResistanceKeyword` matches the current faction model" (section "Data"). The data note verified one sample's names and the package.json exclusion; the stale/current judgement is in its Inferences.
- "**[VERIFIED-ON-DISK]** ... PEP's `explosiveAttack` 'covers projectile explosions but not every radial component'" (same section). The quoted sentence is from the data note's Inferences. The underlying facts hold (this review read Trumna Prime's PEP entry: the primary-fire behaviour is `{DT_IMPACT: 32, DT_FIRE: 53}` with no radial block; the alt-fire has `explosiveAttack {DT_FIRE: 1150}`), but the generalisation is an inference.
- "**[VERIFIED-ON-DISK]** ... Steflos Prime, Nunchasa and Aksondol (released 2026-09-23) are absent from every PEP release including the GitHub head" (same section). The note checked three PEP versions, the GitHub head and DE's live export online (DOCUMENTED, not on disk), and records a release date for only two of the three weapons. The "latest" package versions and publish dates are likewise from npm, not disk.
- The melee worked examples ("120 x 5.05 = 606, x 2.2 = 1,333.2 ... 250 x 3.85 x 6 x 12 = 69,300 ... +33%") sit in a paragraph opening with **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]**; in the melee note they are Inferences. The arithmetic is right; the label is COMPUTED.
- "**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Kuva, Tenet and Coda weapons gain two ranks per polarization to rank 40 after five Forma" (section "Capacity, drain and polarity"). The modding note read only the Kuva (Variant) page and lists Coda rules as not retrieved. The data side is fine: this review counted 52 weapons with `maxLevelCap: 40` in the bundled PEP (24 primaries, 14 secondaries, 12 melee, 2 arch-guns, including Tenet Exec, Paracesis and the Coda weapons).
- "**[DATA-CONFIRMED]** `maxLevelCap: 40` is present on 52 weapons" and the DATA-CONFIRMED label generally. The report defines DATA-CONFIRMED as "read from DE's Public Export files", but `maxLevelCap`, `omegaAttenuation`, `compatibilityTags` and the Riven entries are extras added by the community mirror (`warframe-public-export-plus`), as the modding note says in its source key. They are reliable in practice but are not DE's text.
- "**[OFFICIAL]** Merciless lost its '+100% Ammo Maximum' in Update 32" — the expert note's source is the Primary Merciless wiki page, not a patch note.
- "Non-obvious wiki-stated pairs: ... Creeping Bullseye with Pistol Gambit" (section "Exclusivity"). The modding note says that pair is "not named individually" on the wiki and follows from the corrupted-crit rule.
- "slot cost = ceil(drain / 2) matching polarity (or Universal slot, non-Umbra mod)" — the modding note says Universal-slot halving is "implied by 'Any polarity' wording ... not tested."
- Every CREATOR-STATED quotation is presented as verbatim with a date. The expert note (line 9) says the quotes come from auto-generated YouTube transcripts in which "obvious mis-hearings" were corrected. The numbers quoted (430%, 702%, 8 and 6 procs per kill, 65%, 125%) are the kind a transcript can garble; none was checked against the video.

**Claims that rest on one weak source or an inference, presented as established.**

- "Entropic Eximus" values (section "Damage attenuation ..."). The report rightly says all three notes used a summariser. For what it is worth, this review's own **(query)** read of the Eximus page returned "Secondary weapons is reduced by 90% ... 75% ... Glaives and Gunblades are reduced by 90%", concordant with the enemy-defences note; still two summariser reads, not a verbatim one. It would change a recommendation (melee versus gun) in any Zariman or Entropic-heavy week.
- The whole status-rate model (section "Putting a number on status chance") is labelled RESEARCHER-INFERENCE and then becomes priority item 3 with specific numbers. The arithmetic reproduces (see below), but two presentation choices overstate it. "Heat reaches about 21.6 stacks after 10 s for 5,445 per second" is the instantaneous rate at ten seconds under an unlimited-stacking rule from a wiki section the report itself flags as community-derived; the window average is half that, and the figure is "before armour" although Heat ticks are armour-reduced (545 per second against capped armour unless Heat's own strip is counted). The Viral steady state at 10 stacks assumes several seconds of sustained fire on one target, which the report's own "first shot effect" paragraph says is often not available.
- The 42-fold spread (section "Level scaling ..." and the Conclusion). The arithmetic is right (3,214,850 / 75,643 = 42.5), but the worst case is a neutral element, no status, body shots on a capped Heavy Gunner, which no sensible Steel Path build produces (Corrosive or Impact alone is x1.5 on Grineer; a head is x3), and the best case is a Slash tick under ten Viral stacks, an asymptote reached after seconds of fire. Read as "builds differ by 42x" it is wrong; read as "the target model moves the answer more than any mod does" it is right. The report mostly uses it the second way, the Conclusion the first ("the same eight mods are worth a 42-fold different amount").
- "Guardian Eximus give 90% reduction to area attacks and to nearby allies" labelled WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY (section "Damage attenuation ..."). The endgame note's full read of the Eximus page says "increased damage type resistance to all damage types by 90% for themselves and their allies", and this review's **(query)** read adds "Damage reduction does not apply to Overguard of nearby units." The full-read, broader version was set aside for the summarised narrower one without being listed as a conflict.
- Priority item 8: "Kuva, Tenet and Coda bonus elements and Rivens. Both are already in the inventory in decodable form." The report's own data table says the Lich bonus "value formula UNVERIFIED" and the data note says PEP's `upgradeValues: [{value: 0.1}]` "does not by itself yield 25-60%". The element tag is decodable; the percentage is not yet.
- Priority item 9 recommends replacing the app's mod-family heuristic with the wiki's `Incompatible` lists, which were "read through an extraction tool, not line by line" by one researcher. That is a single tool-mediated read. Two further hazards: the wiki module is keyed by mod name, and the bundled `@wfcd/items` `Mods.json` has three entries named "Serration" (`.../Beginner/...` +40%, `.../Intermediate/...` +90%, the real one +165%), three "Hornet Strike", and "Point Strike" and "Split Chamber" entries at `.../Expert/...` paths (+275% and +165%) that are not the mods players own; any name-keyed join must go through `uniqueName`. PEP's own `incompatibilityTags` (for example Galvanized Crosshairs and Hydraulic Crosshairs carry `NO_AIM`; the Cannonades carry `MODULAR_GUN`) are DE data already on disk and should be used first.
- "Steel Path x2.5" and the Archimedea multiplier are treated as alternatives, never as possibly stacking. The enemy-defences summariser reported "Steel Path multipliers do not apply" to Archimedea; the raw Deep Archimedea page says nothing either way; the Update 40 notes quoted on it speak of "Steel Path or even Temporal Archimedea modifiers" as separate things. Whether an Elite Archimedea enemy is also Steel-Path-flagged is a 2.5x uncertainty on every target in the report's Archimedea table, and it is not on the in-game list.
- "The wiki's CO page says it has 'not been modified for a year'" (section "Gun Condition Overload"). The modding note describes the same page as "last edited 2026-10-05" with an "actively being worked on" banner, and this review's read shows a clean-up banner ("My Condition Overload (Mechanic) page, what have they done to it? Fix this!"). Which statement is right about the page's currency is unresolved, and it bears on how much to trust the per-weapon table.
- Section "Elite Deep Archimedea", modifier table. Values match the wiki **(raw)**, but qualifiers were dropped: Sealed Armor exempts Anatomizers; Elemental Potency also gives enemies "+100% Elemental Damage"; Radioactive Breakdown is a Mirror Defense deviation only; the table has no mission column, so every deviation reads as always possible.
- "Still attenuated: ... and Juggernauts" (section "Damage attenuation"). The endgame note says "Whether Juggernauts, Thralls and Mocking Whispers kept it under the 'HUD health bar' rule is not stated explicitly"; the enemy-defences note infers yes. Stated flatly in the report.
- "Everything else has a structured source" (section "Data", opening) is contradicted by the report's own table rows "none; hand-coded from the wiki formulas", "perk table absent" and "effect values in no export checked".
- "no dataset anywhere carries numeric mod effects per rank" — the data note says "The search was limited to the sources named here; an exhaustive search of GitHub was not done."

3. "474k to 1.12M against 321k" versus the table's 948k full-squad figure. See Error 2.
4. Executive summary "drawn from the player's own inventory" versus the Elite Deep Archimedea section's quotation "can consist of equipment that the player doesn't own". See Error 8.
5. Section "Data" opening ("Everything else has a structured source") versus its own table ("none", "absent", "no export checked").

The bucket section marks Deadhead and Dexterity additive as WIKI-CONFIRMED and the contradictions table says "test Bulwark, Plated Round, Compression"; the modding note's open question 4 also asked about Dexterity and Cascadia Flare, and those two were dropped from the in-game list without a word. The pipeline note's page citations are the better source, but the resolution should be marked SYNTHESIS.

## Changes to the priority list

The ranking's top three are defensible for Steel Path. For Elite Archimedea it under-ranks the items that decide whether the advisor can answer the offered-loadout question at all, and it recommends two things the evidence does not yet support building.

- **Add an item 0: a validation harness.** Every rule past the arsenal number is unchecked in game, and the report's list has 58 open questions but no item for the mechanism that answers them. A fixed Simulacrum protocol (three or four owned guns, a Heavy Gunner, a Crewman, a Murmur unit; body and head; stripped and not) with recorded popups, and a "verified against update N" stamp on each rule (item 15), should precede items 1 to 3, not follow them.
- **Item 1 (target with armour): correct and extend.** Use x2.5 solo for Archimedea; add a toggle for "Steel Path x2.5 also applies" until that is tested; add a light armoured unit (Lancer, 100 armour) to the panel so armour is not uniformly 10x; drop the 42-fold framing in the UI in favour of "effective health by damage source for this target".
- **Move item 6 (per-attack data, trigger types, the CO flag) up to third.** The advisor refuses 30 of 85 guns plus 5 missing; in an Archimedea week the offered weapon is as likely to be a bow or a charge weapon as not, so without this the use case in item 7 does not exist. Item 3's proc-rate formula also needs the effective fire rate and per-pellet multishot this item provides. Default unlisted attacks to adding, not multiplying.
- **Move Rivens (half of item 8) up to directly after item 2.** The decoder exists, the fingerprint formula is reproduced, and a Riven is often the strongest owned mod. Keep the Lich bonus percentage behind an in-game check (the value formula is unverified); the element tag can be used now.
- **Move item 12 (Incarnon forms) above items 9 and 10.** Incarnon guns dominate Steel Path and Archimedea and are frequent offered weapons; scoring Torid or Latron on the base form is the largest single misranking the advisor can make on those weeks. A hand-entered form-stat table for the owned Incarnon guns is cheap even if perks are left out at first.
- **Item 11 (melee) is too low for Elite Archimedea.** It is one of the four loadout rows, exempt from both ammo modifiers, and the report itself calls it "the biggest missing weapon class" there. A coarse model (light-attack damage with Condition Overload, stance multiplier and a chosen combo tier) belongs before exclusivity polish; the full model with Tennokai and arcanes can stay later.
- **Item 9 (wiki `Incompatible` lists): do not build on it yet.** One tool-mediated read; name-keyed against a dataset with duplicate names. Use PEP's `incompatibilityTags` and `compat` paths first, and re-read the wiki module line by line before replacing the family heuristic.
- **Item 7 (Archimedea week inputs): split.** The modifier switches are wiki-confirmed and can be built; the offered-loadout logic must assume unowned offers; the Arsenal Boosts are unknown and should not be modelled; the research-points rule should cite Hotfix 44.0.3, since the wiki pages disagree.
- **Item 2 (conditional stacks and arcanes): keep, with exclusions.** Merciless, Deadhead (additive confirmed here), Galvanized multishot and Scope (individual stack timers) are safe; Bulwark, Plated Round and Compression stay out until their bucket is tested; Dexterity, Cascadia Flare and the Secondary trio are plausible but unquoted. Deadhead's "status proc kills do not count" rule should feed the uptime default for DoT builds.
- **Item 13 (attenuation): keep as a flag only.** The report says so itself; the ranked list reads as if a cap could be computed.
- **Item 14 (handling vetoes) can be pulled earlier as cheap flags.** "Needs weak-point kills", "Internal Bleeding halves above 2.5 fire rate", "Heavy Caliber with a recoil mod" cost little and are the checks the experts actually apply.
- **Missing from the list:** a per-target Overguard share and Magnetic value (partly in 1 and 3, never named); an accuracy or spread assumption for multishot shotguns, since the wiki warns the arsenal "sum of all projectiles" overstates wide-spread weapons; and the Archimedea "SP multiplier stacks?" question.

## Must verify in game before building on it

In order of how much a wrong answer would change a recommendation.

1. **Archimedea health multiplier.** Expect x2.5 solo and x4.0 at four; and whether the Steel Path x2.5 applies on top (a 2.5x swing on every Archimedea target). Also whether Eximus Overguard is multiplied.
2. **Condition Overload per supported weapon.** Adding or multiplying, and whether the additive form ignores Eclipse and range falloff. Expected default: adding.
3. **The Lich bonus percentage** from `UpgradeFingerprint.buffs[0].Value`, against a weapon of known percentage, before item 8 reads it.
4. **Entropic Eximus** reduction against primaries and secondaries (two summariser reads say 90%), and whether punch-through or radial hits enter the orb.
5. **Head-crit doubling with a flat crit-damage source** (base or total multiplier), and which Murmur, Scaldra, Techrot and Fragmented weak points allow the doubling at all.
6. **A 100-armour unit at Steel Path level 175** should show about 1,960 armour, not 2,700. One Codex or Simulacrum reading settles whether the "every armoured enemy is capped" default is safe.
7. **Faction on ticks**: a Slash tick with Primed Bane should be 2.4025x one without; then Rifle Elementalist on the same tick.
8. **Research points and Arsenal Boosts** after Hotfix 44.0.3: one Warframe point per mission, Legendary at 34, and what each boost gives.
9. **Buckets for Bulwark, Plated Round, Compression, the Cannonades, Spectral Serration and the Madurai +25%**, and Plated Round's real formula.
10. **Trumna Prime's primary fire**: 32 Impact and 53 Heat only, or plus a 50 Heat radial; the arsenal's Radial Attack section settles it.
11. **Galvanized Scope and Crosshairs stack timers** (expected individual; cheap to confirm after the 31.6.4 fix) and whether the 44.0.2 description fix matches behaviour.
12. **Secondary Outburst duration** (PEP 30 s, wiki table 20 s) and whether Enervate's +10% is flat.
13. **The damage quantum**, 1/32 or 1/16, with a three-way physical split and a Nullifier bubble as control.
14. **Heat stacking**: whether stacks really grow without limit while refreshed, and which modifiers "Heat inherit" snapshots.
15. **Which Murmur units carry armour**, since the whole "armour strip in Elite Deep Archimedea" dispute turns on it and no researcher obtained the list.
16. **The Deadhead and Galvanized "weak point kill" rule for DoT kills**, which decides the uptime default for Hunter Munitions and Slash builds.
