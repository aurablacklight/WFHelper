A Warframe weapon build is good when it spreads its mods across multiplicative damage buckets, answers the target's defences, and can actually keep its conditional bonuses running, and the current advisor ranks on none of those three. As of Update 44.0.3 (2026-09-30) a gun hit is a product of independent buckets (base damage, elemental and physical, faction, crit, weak point, final multipliers), inside which bonuses only add, so a +360% weapon arcane cuts Serration from a +165% mod to a +36% one. Enemy handling then swings the result by far more than any sensible mod choice: every armoured enemy at Steel Path level sits at the 2,700 armour cap (90% reduction, a tenfold effective-health multiplier), Viral multiplies health damage by up to 4.25, and the researchers computed a 42-fold spread in effective health for one Steel Path Heavy Gunner depending only on armour handling and Viral. Elite Deep and Elite Temporal Archimedea add 2x to 4x health by squad size, Viral-resistant or status-immune targets, weekly modifiers that can zero out a whole build archetype, and three offered weapons per slot drawn from the player's own inventory, which makes "which of these three, with the mods I own, this week" the question an inventory-aware advisor is uniquely placed to answer. The evidence base is uneven and the report says so throughout: the mechanics rest on the official wiki and Digital Extremes' Public Export, both read on 2026-10-05; creator evidence is thirteen video transcripts, only one of them Brozime's, plus Brozime's public vault; no Tactical Potato content and no full Reddit thread could be read, and most researchers were locked out of the official forums. The notes contradict each other in more than a dozen places, and those conflicts are listed and, where possible, resolved. The report ends with a ranked modelling backlog, the rules the app gets wrong today, and the questions only the game can settle.

## How to read the evidence: labels, dates and what nobody could access

Every rule below carries the label the researcher gave it, and the report adds two of its own. **WIKI-CONFIRMED** means the text was read on wiki.warframe.com; **WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY** means a wiki page was read through a summarising fetch tool, so the quoted numbers are probably right but were not seen verbatim; **DATA-CONFIRMED** means the number was read from DE's Public Export files; **VERIFIED-ON-DISK** means a file in the project or the saved inventory was opened; **DOCUMENTED** means a live endpoint was fetched; **OFFICIAL** is a patch note; **CREATOR-STATED** names who said it and when; **COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS** is a named community source; **COMPUTED** is a researcher's arithmetic from wiki formulas; **DISPUTED**, **UNKNOWN** and **UNVERIFIED** mean what they say. **RESEARCHER-INFERENCE** marks a conclusion a researcher drew that no source states, and **SYNTHESIS** marks a judgement made in writing this report by comparing notes. Unless a different date is given, every wiki page and data file was read on 2026-10-05 against live game version Update 44.0 "Iceblade of Narin" (2026-09-23), hotfix 44.0.3 (2026-09-30) ([Update 44 notes](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1523956-update-44-iceblade-of-narin/)).

The access failures matter because they fall exactly where the app's owner places his trust. No Tactical Potato video, written note or transcript was read by any researcher; one researcher found only titles and dates, and concluded his channel is news and single-item showcases with no retrievable methodology ([channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCHAenboNPQJmNQmvQDQibQA)). Brozime's public Obsidian vault was read and is thin on weapons: its one modding page is a video embed and says "I won't be putting the information in the video into text here" ([Basic Modding Overview](https://publish.obsidian.md/brozime/Public/Guides+%26+Builds/Modding/Basic+Modding+Overview)); only one Brozime transcript was read, a new-player guide dated 2023-12-09, which predates the Update 36 damage rework. TheKengineer is the only creator whose method is documented here in depth, through transcripts dated 2020 to 2024; his 2025 "Utility Primaries" video, his Steel Path, AoE-meta and Archon Shard videos, and his three mythbusting videos were blocked. YouTube began IP-blocking transcript requests partway through, so about twenty relevant videos were not read, and one long 2025 modding guide by NoSympathyy was downloaded and left unread. Reddit blocked every direct fetch, so every Reddit opinion below is a one- or two-sentence search excerpt with no thread, date or vote count behind it. The official forums returned HTTP 403 to three researchers (enemy defences, status effects, endgame content); the damage-pipeline and melee researchers did read a handful of forum threads. ## One hit is a product of eight buckets, and the arsenal shows only three

### The order of operations

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The arsenal number covers base damage, elemental and physical mods, and multishot; "this arsenal damage is the average non-crit damage per shot, without Faction Damage Bonus" ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation)). Faction damage, crits, weak points, final multipliers, falloff and beam ramp-up all come after it. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The general stacking form is `Stat = Base x PRODUCT(1 + SUM of additive bonuses per bucket) + SUM of flat bonuses`, with internal operation types ADD, STACKING_MULTIPLY, MULTIPLY and SET ([Calculating Bonuses](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Calculating_Bonuses)). The damage-pipeline researcher assembled the wiki's statements into one expression per projectile, before enemy defences; the assembly is **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]**, each line of it is **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]**:

```
ModdedBase = (Base + flat base adds) x (1 + SUM base-damage bonuses)          bucket A
share_i    = quantize( type i's proportion of ModdedBase )                    bucket B
             physical type: base share x (1 + SUM that type's mods)
             element:       innate share + SUM that element's mod %
D_i        = share_i x ModdedBase
PreCrit    = SUM_i D_i x (1 + enemy faction modifier_i)
Hit        = PreCrit
             x (1 + SUM "multiplying" gun Condition Overload)   bucket C (projectile weapons)
             x (1 + SUM faction bonuses)                        bucket D
             x CritMult(tier, weak point)                       bucket E
             x WeakPointMult                                    bucket F
             x PRODUCT of final multipliers                     bucket G
             x RangeFalloff x BeamRampUp                        bucket H
             x enemy vulnerability x (1 - armour DR)
Expected damage per trigger pull = Hit x Multishot
```

The worked example from the notes shows how far the real number sits from the arsenal's. A hitscan rifle with 100 base damage of one physical type carries Serration (+165%), Primary Merciless at 12 stacks (+360%), two 90% Heat mods and Primed Bane of Grineer (+55%), with 200% crit chance and a 4.4 crit multiplier. ModdedBase is 100 x (1 + 1.65 + 3.60) = 625. The Heat share of 1.80 quantizes to 58/32 = 1.8125, so the total is 625 x 2.8125 = 1,757.81 where the arsenal would show a smooth 1,750. Faction multiplies by 1.55 to 2,724.61. A body-shot orange crit multiplies by 1 + 2 x (4.4 - 1) = 7.8 for 21,252; a headshot orange crit on a Grineer multiplies by 3 x [1 + 2 x (2 x 4.4 - 1)] = 49.8 for 135,686. The arsenal number is 1,750; the real hit is between twelve and seventy-eight times that, and none of the difference is visible to a calculator that stops at the arsenal ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation), [Critical Hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit)).

### What shares Serration's bucket, and why that is the central modding fact

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Primary and Secondary Merciless (+30% x 12 = 360%, 4 s per stack), Deadhead (+120% x 3 = 360%, 24 s) and Dexterity (+60% x 6 = 360%, 20 s, on melee kill) all state "the damage bonus stacks additively with other damage mods like Serration" ([Primary Merciless](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Merciless), [Primary Deadhead](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Deadhead)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** per the pipeline note, the same bucket holds Arcane Rage (+180% primary) and Arcane Precision (+300% secondary), Arcane Primary Charger (+300%), Cascadia Flare (12% x 40 = 480%), Primary Bulwark (1% per armour point over 1,000, cap 500%), Primary Plated Round (15 x sqrt(5 x magazine) %) and Vex Armor's Fury ([Cascadia Flare](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Cascadia_Flare), [Primary Bulwark](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Bulwark), [Primary Plated Round](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Plated_Round), [Vex Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vex_Armor)). **The notes contradict each other here.** The modding researcher writes that "only Merciless is explicitly wiki-confirmed", that Deadhead, Dexterity and Cascadia Flare are "almost certainly" additive by wording, that Primary Bulwark's bucket is UNKNOWN, and that Plated Round's formula "is not in" the Public Export text. The pipeline researcher cites the individual wiki pages for each; the expert-heuristics researcher independently quotes the Deadhead page's additive sentence. **[SYNTHESIS]** The pipeline note is the better-sourced on Deadhead and Dexterity; Bulwark and Plated Round rest on one researcher's read of one page each and belong on the in-game list.

**[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer put the same structure in a video dated 2021-08-01: "all mods and most bonuses are additive to the respective base value"; Serration plus Heavy Caliber is 100 + 165 + 165 = 430%, "only 62 percent higher than the 265 total... This is why additive bonuses are considered the weakest type", whereas Serration plus a 165% elemental is 265% x 265% = 702%. His "pure damage" bucket contains "damage mods like Serration, corrupted damage mods like Heavy Caliber, nightmare mods like Blaze, arcanes..., status overload mods like Condition Overload, some abilities like Vex Armor or Shooting Gallery, even... Helminth invigorations; all of these modifiers are added together" ([Damage calculation guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N3n89cZpVTg)). The video predates Update 36, but the stacking rules it states match the current wiki pages above. **[COMPUTED]** With Merciless at 360% the bucket is 4.60; adding Serration makes it 6.25, a 35.9% gain. The same slot spent on a first 90% elemental mod gives +90% to direct damage, and on Primed Bane +55% to direct hits and +140% to damage-over-time ticks.

Three other families sit outside that bucket. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Final multipliers named by the wiki are Eclipse, Primed Chamber, the sniper combo, Longbow Sharpshot (+300%), Primary Compression on projectile weapons, Extinguished Dragon Key, Hall of Mirrors, Parasitic Link, Vauban's Overdriver, Duality, Furious Javelin and Garuda's passive ([Condition Overload (Mechanic)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic))). Primed Chamber is +100% on the first shot of a magazine "applied multiplicatively after all other modifiers from mods and abilities" ([Primed Chamber](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primed_Chamber)); the sniper combo is 1.5x at the first tier and +0.5x per tier ([Sniper Rifle](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Sniper_Rifle)). Secondary Surge is a separate multiplier, described as "up to +700% on next shot" in the pipeline note and as "up to x8" in the modding note, which are the same number in two notations ([Secondary Surge](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Secondary_Surge)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Ability-added elements behave as an extra elemental mod in bucket B: Nourish adds 75% Viral (45% subsumed) "additive with existing elemental and IPS mods", Smite Infusion +100% Radiation ([Nourish](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Nourish), [Smite Infusion](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Smite_Infusion)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Xata's Whisper (26% of total weapon damage as Void) and Toxic Lash (30% on guns as Toxin) are separate damage instances that do not dilute the weapon's elements; Xata's Whisper "double dips on faction damage, and body part weaknesses" ([Xata's Whisper](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Xata%27s_Whisper), [Toxic Lash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Toxic_Lash)). **[UNKNOWN]** The bucket for the Cannonade mods (+240% rifle and shotgun, +300% pistol, Update 36), Spectral Serration (+330% while invisible, Update 38) and the Update 41 Madurai "+25% Primary and Secondary Damage" is not stated in any source read.

### Gun Condition Overload is three different mods depending on the attack

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED, from a community-tested page flagged UpdateMe]** Galvanized Aptitude and Savvy give +40% direct damage per status type on the target per stack, two stacks, 20 s (80% per status); Galvanized Shot gives 40% x 3 stacks for 14 s (120% per status). On most hitscan attacks the bonus adds to Serration: `Base x [(1 + Serration etc.) x other multipliers + CO]`. On most projectile attacks it multiplies: `Base x (1 + Serration) x (1 + CO)`. On explosion radius damage it does nothing; the Galvanized Aptitude page records an Update 30.9 patch note (2021-11-11) that this is by design ([Condition Overload (Mechanic)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic)), [Galvanized Aptitude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Aptitude)). **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer, 2024-05-01: "Projectiles usually use multiplicative CO... Hitscan usually use additive CO, additive specifically to pure damage modifiers like Serration... Radial usually doesn't use CO at all... All three of these I have to stress usually because variations exist" ([Is Roar always better than Eclipse?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUMPxnpmtKQ)). **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS]** PublikDomain on the official forums, 2021-07-09, gives the additive form "165% + (40% x 2) x 5 = 565% Damage" ([forum thread](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1270893-galvanized-aptitude-additive-or-multiplicative-damage/)).

The worked comparison, for a 100-base weapon with Serration, two Aptitude stacks and two status types on the target (CO = +160%): hitscan 100 x (1 + 1.65 + 1.60) = 425; projectile 100 x 2.65 x 2.60 = 689; hitscan with 12-stack Merciless 100 x (1 + 1.65 + 3.60 + 1.60) = 785, at which point Serration contributes 165/620 = 26.6%. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** In the additive form the CO part skips final multipliers, range falloff (not beam ramp-up), Incarnon Genesis flat base damage and bow charge multipliers; so the same hitscan weapon under full-strength Eclipse (x3) deals 100 x (2.65 x 3 + 1.60) = 955, not 425 x 3 = 1,275. The bonus is "not applied on the hit that procs a new status effect", up to 16 status types can count, and statuses from any source count ([Condition Overload (Mechanic)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic)), [Damage Falloff](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Falloff)). The wiki's own multishot example shows the ordering effect: 100 base, Serration, two stacks, multishot 3 deals 265 then 345 then 425, a total of 1,035 instead of 795 ([Galvanized Aptitude](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Aptitude)).

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED, community-sourced tables]** Per-weapon exceptions break the hitscan/projectile rule: Arca Plasmor, Aeolak, Alternox, Basmu, Battacor, Bubonico, Buzlok, Exergis, Felarx, Fulmin semi mode, Hema, Javlok, Cinta, Dread Incarnon and Latron Incarnon multiply; charged bows (Cernos, Dread, Daikyu, Paris) get 50% of the listed bonus because CO uses uncharged damage; Acceltra gets 74.3%. Secondary Shiver (+45% per Cold stack, up to 450%), Cedo (+60% per status) and several Incarnon perks (Latron +30%, Soma, Zylok, Angstrum, Despair +40%) share the CO bucket and add to each other ([Condition Overload (Mechanic)](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload_(Mechanic)), [Secondary Shiver](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Secondary_Shiver)). **[DISPUTED]** An undated Steam snippet says flatly "Aptitude is a multiplicative damage", and a low-trust Overframe Nataruk build claims multiplicative behaviour on charged shots ([Overframe build](https://overframe.gg/build/279107/nataruk/one-shot-steel-path-150k-slash-procs-180-crit-chance-2-forma/)); both contradict the wiki's general case and only the second names a specific attack. **[UNKNOWN]** The wiki's CO page says it has "not been modified for a year", and no researcher extracted the full per-attack catalogue, so behaviour for most weapons the app supports is unverified. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** A calculator needs a per-attack flag (adding, multiplying, none, partial percentage); guessing from hitscan versus projectile gets the bows, Acceltra and at least fifteen other weapons wrong.

### Critical hits: tiers, quantization and the head rule that depends on faction

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Crit chance is `Base x (1 + SUM relative) + SUM absolute`; the wiki's Braton example is 12% x (1 + 1.5 + 1.35) = 46.2%, or 12% x 2.5 + 45% = 75% with Arcane Avenger's flat +45%. Crit damage has the same form; Braton 1.6 x (1 + 1.2 + 1.2) = 5.44. The tier is the integer part of crit chance, plus one with probability equal to the fraction; the tier multiplier is `1 + Tier x (CD - 1)`; the average multiplier `1 + CC x (CD - 1)` is linear and holds at any crit chance (Paris with Point Strike and Vital Sense: 1 + 0.75 x 3.4 = 3.55). Each pellet rolls its own crit, and multishot does not touch crit stats ([Critical Hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit), [Update 27](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Update_27)). Flat crit chance sources are Arcane Avenger (+45%), Cat's Eye, Covenant, Puncture status, Secondary Enervate (+10% per hit) and Shadow Haze; flat crit damage sources are Arcane Crepuscular, Cold status, Shroud of Dynar and Tenacious Bond. **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer's corollary, 2021-08-01: "absolute buffs are stronger the lower the base and modded values are" (Kuva Nukor: 7% base, 20.1% with Primed Pistol Gambit, 65.1% with Avenger) ([Damage calculation guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N3n89cZpVTg)).

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The base crit multiplier is quantized to a 12-bit code before mods: `Code = floor(clamp(x, 0, 32) x 4095/32 + 0.5)`, decoded as `Code x 32/4095`; a base of 2.0 becomes 2.000488, so with +120% the multiplier is 4.4011 rather than 4.4 ([Critical Hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit)). This is a fourth-decimal effect, relevant only to matching Simulacrum numbers.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** A critical headshot on an eligible head doubles the crit multiplier inside the tier formula: `Headshot Multi x (1 + Tier x (2 x CD - 1))`; a Lanka orange crit is 3.0 x (1 + 2 x (2 x 4.4 - 1)) = 49.8x. Eligible heads are Grineer (including Corrupted, Narmer and Anarch Grineer), Dax Anarchs, Scaldra, most Infested and most Sentients. It "excludes the heads of all Corpus humanoids and ground units, most of The Murmur, and much of Techrot", and it applies to heads, not to every weak point ([Enemy Body Parts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts), [Critical Hit](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Critical_Hit)). The enemy-defences note's worked case makes the size of this plain: a 5.0x crit multiplier at tier 1 gives 5x on the body, 3 x (1 + (2 x 5 - 1)) = 30x on a Grineer head, 3 x 5 = 15x on a Corpus Crewman head, and 7.5x on a Murmur 1.5x weak point. **[DISPUTED, wording inside the wiki]** Critical Hit doubles the total crit multiplier; Enemy Body Parts says the bonus "applies a 2x multiplier to the weapon's base critical mult". The two agree with relative mods only and diverge when a flat crit damage bonus is active (Cold freeze, Tenacious Bond, Arcane Crepuscular). **[UNKNOWN]** Which is right.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The Vigilante set gives a 5% chance per equipped set mod (30% with all six) to raise a primary weapon's crit by one tier ([Vigilante Set](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Vigilante_Set)); **[DATA-CONFIRMED]** the Public Export text reads "5% / 10% / 15% / 20% / 25% / 30% chance to enhance Critical Hits from Primary Weapons." **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** The expected value below 100% crit chance, `1 + CC x (1 + p) x (CD - 1)`, is the pipeline researcher's derivation from the wording that only crits are enhanced: at 50% crit chance, 4.4 crit damage and six mods it gives 3.21 against 2.70 without. The modding researcher records the same mechanic's exact behaviour as "not retrieved". **[CREATOR-STATED, status UNKNOWN]** TheKengineer showed in 2020 that Vigilante mods on a sentinel weapon counted for the player's primary ([Top 10 tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n8ACICukYEk)); nobody checked whether that survives.

### Weak points are two additive brackets multiplied together

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** `WeakPointMult = (BodyPartMult + k x SUM weak point damage bonuses) x (1 + SUM headshot multiplier bonuses)`, with k = 1.5 if the weak point is a head and 1 otherwise. Weak point damage bonuses are the Acuity mods (+350%) and Seek; headshot multiplier bonuses are Deadhead (+30%), Target Acquired (+60%), sniper zoom and Prowl. Acuity plus Deadhead on a 3x head is (3 + 1.5 x 3.5) x 1.3 = 10.725x; Seek plus Acuity plus Deadhead and a 0.4 zoom bonus is 9.375 x 1.7 = 15.94x. The default head multiplier is 3.0x for Grineer, Corpus, Infested, Sentient, Anarchs, Narmer and Corrupted; "for The Murmur and Techrot, the usual multiplier is closer to 1.5x". Weapons with a forced 1x headshot multiplier (Arca Plasmor, Catchmoon, Fulmin semi, Ignis, Nataruk charged shots, Dread, Lex and Paris Incarnon forms) are "unaffected by acuity-like bonuses but affected by deadhead-like bonuses" ([Enemy Body Parts](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Body_Parts), [Primary Acuity](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Primary_Acuity)). **[DISPUTED inside the wiki]** Damage/Calculation gives two incompatible forms in different rows, and Calculating Bonuses still uses the pre-Update-32 2x headshot; the pipeline researcher prefers Enemy Body Parts as the current, detailed rule. **[OFFICIAL]** Update 32 (2022-09-07) raised the headshot multiplier from 2x to 3x and made radial damage unable to headshot. **[OFFICIAL]** Update 44 (2026-09-23) turned many "On Headshot" triggers into "On Weakpoint" (Deadhead, Argon and Galvanized Scope, Hydraulic and Galvanized Crosshairs, Arcane Precision, Longbow Sharpshot), made Sonar spots count as weak points, and made Bursa and most MOA rear hitboxes non-headshot weak points, so weak-point bonuses apply there at the 1x rate with no headshot-multiplier bonus and no head-crit doubling ([Update 44 notes](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1523956-update-44-iceblade-of-narin/)). **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Murmur and Techrot heads (about 1.5x, no crit doubling) make headshot-focused crit builds roughly a quarter as effective there as against Grineer, which bears directly on Elite Deep Archimedea.

### Faction damage is linear on hits and squared on ticks

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Faction damage is "calculated as a total damage multiplier": Bane mods +30%, Primed +55%, Roar 50% (30% subsumed) and the relay Damage Blessing 25%, all additive with each other and multiplicative with everything else; the wiki's example is 100 x 2.65 x (1 + 0.5 + 0.3) = 477. Bane mods do not affect Corrupted or Narmer variants, and no faction mods exist for Anarchs, Narmer, Scaldra, Techrot, Stalker or Wild ([Faction Damage Bonus](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Faction_Damage_Bonus)). **[DATA-CONFIRMED]** per the modding note, faction mods exist for Corpus, Grineer, Infested, Orokin and The Murmur, with Primed Bane of The Murmur introduced 2025-09-02. The bonus is applied a second time to damage-over-time ticks: a Slash tick is `Base x (1 + Serration) x 0.35 x (1 + Faction)^2 x (1 + Status Damage)`. **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS]** Kaomos on the forums, 2025-04-16: "bane mods double dip on damaging statuses, 1.55x damage on the initial hit, 2.4025x status damage" ([forum thread](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1451329-universal-bane-mod-and-the-issues-surrounding-faction-damage-multipliers/)). **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer, 2024-05-01: "Faction damage is notoriously powerful due to being applied twice multiplicatively when dealing status damage." With Primed Bane and full Roar a direct hit is x2.05 and a Slash tick x4.20; adding Rifle Elementalist's +90% status damage makes the tick x7.98. **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS, single source, unverified]** Kaomos also claims a "triple dip" of 7.075x through extra-hit effects such as Xata's Whisper and Toxic Lash; the wiki supports only the double dip. **[OFFICIAL, meaning UNKNOWN]** Update 29.10 (2021-03-19) says "Fixed the Bane Mods applying the increased Damage twice"; the DoT double dip is still described by the 2026 wiki, so that fix was something else.

### Multishot, quantization, trigger types and radial damage

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Multishot is `base projectile count x (1 + bonus)`; the integer part is guaranteed projectiles and the fraction is the chance of one more, at no ammo cost, with crit and status rolled per projectile. Lex with +180% fires 2.8; Hek with Hell's Chamber fires 15.4 ([Multishot](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Multishot)). The arsenal status chance is per pellet since Update 27.2 (2020-03-05). Beams are the exception: "additional beams that hit the same target instead merge into a singular damage tick" with summed damage and summed status chance but the crit chance of one beam, so DoT output "is affected twice by multishot"; forced procs such as Hunter Munitions apply once per merged tick. **[COMPUTED]** A beam at 40% status and 2.5 multishot scales expected DoT with E[n^2] = 6.5 against 2.5 for a non-beam weapon. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Beam damage ramps from 20% to 100% over 0.6 s on a target for most weapons, decays from 0.8 s after it stops hitting, and consumes 0.5 ammo per tick on most beams ([Continuous Weapon](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Continuous_Weapon)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The Acuity mods SET multishot to the weapon's base value, ignoring even Riven penalties.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Each damage type is rounded to a fixed fraction of modded base damage before other multipliers: `Quantized(x) = sign(x) x floor(|x| x 32 + 0.5) / 32`, where x is the type's value divided by modded base. A 30/30/40 Impact/Puncture/Slash split becomes 31.25/31.25/40.625 = 103.125 (+3.1%); a +90% elemental becomes 90.625%, a +60% becomes 59.375%. A type below 1.5625% of base rounds to zero and can never proc. DoT ticks use unquantized modded base ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation)). **[DISPUTED]** PublikDomain's 2024 in-game tests matched a quantum of 1/16, quoting the wiki as it then read ("100 / 16 = 6.25"), and a Reddit snippet agrees ([forum thread](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1403973-when-does-damage-quantization-and-rounding-apply-and-what-parts-are-quantized-and-rounded/)). **The notes contradict each other on why.** The pipeline researcher "found no source explaining the change"; the enemy-defences researcher writes that 1/32 was "changed from 1/16 in U40, undocumented" and cites nothing. **[SYNTHESIS]** The Update 40 attribution is an unsupported assertion and should not be repeated as fact; the quantum is the single most cheaply testable discrepancy in the whole research. Either way the effect is under 1% for single-element builds and up to about 3% on three-way physical splits (double that at 1/16).

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Effective fire rate depends on trigger. Auto, semi, duplex, held and spooled auto-spool weapons use the modded fire rate; charge weapons use `1 / (modded charge time + 1 / modded fire rate)`; burst weapons use `Burst Count / (1 / modded fire rate + (Burst Count - 1) x Burst Delay)`; bows use `1 / (modded charge time + modded reload time)`. Time stats divide: `Reload = Base / (1 + bonus)`. Semi-auto weapons cap at 10 rounds per second, fire rate cannot fall below 0.05, and "Burst Delay is not affected by net negative Fire Rate bonuses". Sustained DPS is `Burst DPS x Shots / (Effective Fire Rate x Reload Time + Shots)` ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation), [Fire Rate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Fire_Rate), [Reload](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Reload)). Worked: a 3-round burst at fire rate 2.5 with 0.05 s delay fires 3 / (0.4 + 0.1) = 6.0 shots per second; an auto weapon with average shot 1,000, fire rate 10, magazine 60 and a 2 s reload has 10,000 burst and 60 / (20 + 60) = 0.75 uptime, so 7,500 sustained; a 100-round beam at 0.5 ammo per tick and fire rate 12 has 200 / (24 + 200) = 0.893 uptime. Ammo efficiency gives `1 / (1 - efficiency)` shots per round ([Ammo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Ammo)). **[DISPUTED inside the wiki]** The Bow page says bows "lack a Reload delay" while the bow fire-rate formula uses reload time. **[UNKNOWN]** How fractional magazine sizes round.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** "You can treat any direct hits as having two separate instances of damage: direct hit and the AoE hit." The explosion has its own stats, linear falloff from the centre with a per-weapon percentage (Acceltra 50%, Kuva Ogris 80%, Kuva Tonkor 70%, Lenz 70%, Zarr 50%), "a 1x headshot multiplier and cannot trigger headshot conditions", no gun Condition Overload, a status roll per enemy, and it ignores line of sight. Self-damage was replaced by self-stagger in Update 27.2 ([Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation), [Area of Effect](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Area_of_Effect)). For a launcher with 100 direct and 800 radial at 70% falloff over 6 m, the directly hit enemy takes 100 x (body and crit multipliers) + 800, an enemy 3 m away takes 800 x (1 - 0.7 x 3/6) = 520, and one at the edge takes 240; a headshot triples only the 100. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Weak-point mods and CO mods are therefore close to worthless on launchers and should be scored against the direct portion only. **[UNKNOWN]** There is no general rule for whether the radial instance scales with multishot; the wiki lists only weapons where it does not (Ambassador alt-fire, Ferrox, Glaxion Vandal, Braton and Burston Incarnon radial).

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Range falloff is linear between two distances down to a per-weapon minimum: 200 damage with 10 to 30 m falloff and 80% maximum reduction deals 160 at 15 m, 120 at 20 m and 40 beyond 30 m. Punch through carries no stated damage penalty. The only true player-side caps are the fire-rate limits, status stack caps, and stack limits on arcanes and Galvanized mods; everything else called "diminishing returns" is opportunity cost inside an additive bucket, where adding X% to a bucket already at S is worth X / (1 + S) ([Damage Falloff](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Falloff), [Punch Through](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Punch_Through), [Calculating Bonuses](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Calculating_Bonuses)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The stealth damage bonus (+700% at rank 30) is described only for standard melee attacks; **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** treat it as melee-only.

The swing between worst and best element is at most 3x, which is why the enemy-defences researcher ranks it below armour and Viral, and why element choice should be decided mainly by the status effect.

### Armour: a square-root curve with a cap everything reaches

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED, raw LaTeX]** For net armour up to 2,700, `Enemy DR = 0.9 x sqrt(NetArmor / 2700)`. "Enemy armor produced by level scaling is hard capped at 2,700, granting 90% Damage Reduction"; "Steel Path no longer increases Armor values"; minimum initial armour is 200; armour reduces damage to health only, "not to shields or overguard" ([Armor](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Armor), [Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction)). **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer states the same curve as "armor x 3, square rooted, then divided by 100" (2024-06-26), which is algebraically identical; the expert-heuristics researcher's own wiki fetch lost the square root in rendering and raised it as an open question, which the raw-LaTeX read settles. **[COMPUTED]** A Heavy Gunner (500 base armour) at level 175 scales to 9,791 raw armour and is capped to 2,700; a 100-base-armour unit reaches roughly 1,960 to 2,700 at Steel Path levels. All Grineer, Scaldra, armoured Techrot, "some Murmur" and most bosses have armour; "normal Corpus and Infested enemies do not have any" except Bursas, Oxium Ospreys and the Juggernaut.

Strip sources differ in what they act on. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Corrosive status removes 26% at the first stack and 6% per stack to 80% at ten, for 8 s, on current armour; Heat removes 50% of current armour, ramping 15/30/40/50% over 2 s; Corrosive Projection removes 18% of total per aura (72% with four); ability strips and Unairu Caustic Strike remove a percentage of total. The Corrosive page gives the combination explicitly: `Armour remaining = (1 - 50% if Heat) x [1 - (20% + 6% x Corrosive stacks)] x (1 - 18% x Corrosive Projections)` ([Corrosive](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Corrosive_Damage), [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage)). The enemy-defences researcher listed "do Heat and Corrosive multiply?" as an assumption and an open question; the status-effects researcher's read of that formula answers it: they multiply. From the cap:

| State | Net armour | DR | Damage gain vs unstripped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No strip | 2,700 | 90.0% | 1.00x |
| 1 Corrosive Projection | 2,214 | 81.5% | 1.85x |
| 1 Corrosive stack | 1,998 | 77.4% | 2.26x |
| Heat proc | 1,350 | 63.6% | 3.64x |

The curve's shape carries the modelling consequence: the first 50% of strip is worth 3.6x and the last 20% only 1.67x. **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer says the same in words: one Corrosive proc on a max-armour enemy "will provide you with a 125% damage bonus", and post-Update-36 "builds relying on armor stripping or slash procs have become weaker while builds brute forcing through the armor have come out ahead" (2024-06-26 and 2024-07-11, [level scaling video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CgYBFOiSRPQ)). **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Emerald Archon Shards raise the Corrosive cap by 2 (3 Tauforged) each, so 14 stacks fully strip.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** What bypasses what: Slash procs bypass armour but not shields (since Update 27.2) and hit Overguard; Toxin, direct and proc, bypasses ordinary shields but is reduced by armour; True damage bypasses both; "each damage type has a minimum damage of 1" against armour ([Damage](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage), [Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield)). Against a capped target a Slash tick is worth ten times a same-sized Heat, Toxin, Electricity or Gas tick. **[WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY]** Kuva Lich armour "cannot be stripped"; **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Archons are "immune to armor reduction" ([Archon Hunt](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Archon_Hunt)).

### Shields, the gate and Overguard

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Enemy shields take full damage with no armour and no innate reduction. "Enemies have a shield gate that lasts 0.1 seconds, during which only 5% of the damage dealt will damage their health. However, targeting weakspots will completely bypass their shield gate"; slam-type area attacks can be blocked entirely; status ticks and Xata's Whisper still land. Steel Path multiplies shields by 2.5 (was 6.25 before Update 36). Treasurers and Hounds cannot be Toxin-bypassed ([Shield](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Shield)). **[COMPUTED]** A Corpus Tech at Steel Path level 175 has 384,525 health and 59,787 shields, so shields are 13% of the pool. A 500,000-damage body shot with no Toxin breaks the shield, has its remaining 440,213 gated to 5% (22,011), and leaves the Tech at 94% health; the same shot to the head kills. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Shields therefore matter through the gate, not their size: a calculator should treat a shielded target as needing at least two body hits without Toxin, not add shields to effective health. **[UNKNOWN]** Whether the 5% applies to the whole hit or only the overflow.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Overguard sits above shields and health. "All Eximus units have a base Overguard of 12" scaling by level only (`1 + 260 x (level - 1)^0.9` above level 50), identical across Eximus types; it is neutral to every damage type except Void (+50%), "is not affected by Damage Reduction from armor or abilities", and "Damage Vulnerability applies normally". While it is up the enemy ignores stagger, knockdown, stun, mind control, confusion including Radiation procs, slow, ragdoll, blind and Lifted, takes at most 4 Cold procs, but still receives status effects and can be armour-stripped underneath ([Overguard](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Overguard)). **[COMPUTED]** Eximus Overguard is 324,090 at level 175, 683,971 at level 400 and 836,472 at level 500. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Viral does nothing to Overguard (fixed Update 31.6, 2022-06-09); Magnetic status multiplies damage to shields and Overguard by 2x at one stack up to 4.25x at ten and, on break, deals Electricity equal to 3% of the maximum per stack up to 30%. Secondary Fortifier deals up to 8x to Overguard. **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer flagged the on-break damage as bugged in June 2024; whether it was fixed is **[UNKNOWN]**. **[UNKNOWN]** Whether the Steel Path 2.5x applies to Overguard, and whether crits, weak points and faction mods apply to it normally (two researchers assumed yes; the page gives no rule).

### Level scaling, the mode multipliers and the reference targets

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Health and shields follow `Multiplier = f1 + (f2 - f1) x smoothstep((d - 70)/10)` with d the level above base level. The high-level health curve is `1 + 10.7332 x d^0.72` for Grineer and Scaldra, `1 + 13.4165 x d^0.55` for Corpus, `1 + 16.0998 x d^0.72` for Infested, `1 + 10.7332 x d^0.685` for Corrupted, `1 + 10.7332 x d^0.5` for Murmur and Sentient, and `1 + 15.0998 x d^0.7` for Techrot. Eximus base health is replaced before scaling (above level 100: `max(1.1 x Base, 0.25 x (Base + 900) x 6)` for units with shields or armour). Regular enemies do not scale with squad size; Demolishers, Acolytes and Archons do ([Enemy Level Scaling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Enemy_Level_Scaling)). **[DISPUTED inside the page]** Anarchs are grouped with Corrupted in the tab list and with Murmur in the formula caption. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED, full read]** Steel Path is "+100 levels" (+50 on Archwing and Railjack) and "an additional +150% (to a total of 250%, or 2.5x) bonus to health and shields", with no armour multiplier ([The Steel Path](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/The_Steel_Path)).

**The two notes that cover Archimedea health contradict each other, and the endgame note wins.** The enemy-defences researcher read the Archimedea pages through a summariser, could not tell whether a health multiplier applied, and published two speculative columns ("no multiplier" and "x2.5"). The maximum is therefore x4.0 in a full squad, and the "no multiplier" column is wrong. The solo value is less certain than the endgame note makes it sound: the note says "x2.0 solo up to x4.0 in a four-player squad" and then lists "x2.0 solo, x2.5, x3.0, x3.5 ... up to x4.0 at four players", which is five values for four squad sizes. If the 50% is counted per additional member, solo is x2.0; if the solo player counts as a squad member, solo is x2.5 (in which case the enemy-defences "x2.5" column happens to be the solo figure). **[SYNTHESIS, recomputed from the two notes]** Applying the confirmed range to the enemy-defences base figures:

### Damage attenuation is a cap proportional to health, and it now applies only to true bosses

**[OFFICIAL, quoted on the wiki's Damage Reduction page]** Update 40 (2025-10-15) standardised attenuation on the Archon Hunt model: a Max Damage Per Instance cap and a Max Damage Per Second cap, both "now proportional to Max Health" (health only), with the per-second cap "applied on a per-player basis". Hotfix 40.0.2 (2025-10-16): "Damage Attenuation is now solely reserved for true Bosses and enemies with the HUD health bars", removing it from Demolishers including Necramech Demolishers, Rogue Necramechs, Amalgams, Scaldra Dedicants, Techrot Babau, Gruzzlings, Necramites, Sister Hounds, the Treasurer and Tusk Thumpers. Hotfix 40.0.3 (2025-10-21) raised the per-instance cap on Liches, Sisters and Technocyte Coda "so that weapons like Snipers are more effective" and "greatly increased" it for Eidolons ([Damage Reduction](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage_Reduction), [Update 40 notes](https://www.warframe.com/en/patch-notes/xbox/40-0-0)). Still attenuated: Archons, Acolytes (tuned so one-shots remain possible; base health 5,500), adversaries, Eidolons, Void Angels including the Archimedea revival Angel, The Fragmented One, Legacyte, the H-09 tank, Lephantis and Hemocyte, and Juggernauts. Published ratios exist for only two: Eidolons (per-hit cap 200% of max health, per-second 100%) and Lephantis/Hemocyte (per-hit 5%, Hemocyte per-second 20%). The only published closed form is the Hemocyte one, `Attenuated = Incoming x Cap / (Incoming x Multishot + Cap)`; the wiki's example is 12,000 incoming at multishot 2 against a 123,610 cap, landing as 10,049 per projectile, and a 1,000,000 hit lands as 110,014. **[DISPUTED, probably stale]** The wiki still prints flat Archon values (460,000 per trigger, 935,000 per second, decay constant 2/3, shotgun compensation 0.7, a flat 20% reduction and 50% status-damage reduction) and flat Fragmented One values (175,000 and 300,000) under a banner saying the page needs a rewrite; they conflict with "proportional to Max Health". **[UNKNOWN]** The proportional ratios for every Archimedea-relevant boss. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Both the enemy-defences and endgame researchers independently conclude that against attenuated bosses the advisor should report "reaches the cap or not" and penalise wasted single-hit size, not rank by DPS; the suggestion that the standard model has the Hemocyte formula's hyperbolic shape is the enemy-defences researcher's inference alone.

Other defences that reshape builds: **[WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY]** Nullifier bubbles count each hit as 100 to 400 damage (300 to 1,200 with Magnetic), so fire rate beats damage; Guardian Eximus give 90% reduction to area attacks and to nearby allies; Arctic Eximus globes (reworked Update 42, 2026-03-25) block ranged fire from outside; Arbitration drones make tethered enemies invulnerable; Ancient Healers cleanse status. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Sentients adapt at four health gates with 90/80/75/70% resistance to the dominant type. **Three notes disagree on the new Entropic Eximus** (Update 44): the enemy-defences note gives 90% reduction against primaries and secondaries, 75% against Amps and 90% against ranged melee, with a 44.0.2 fix for double application; the status note quotes a 44.0.1 line "Damage from non-melee sources is reduced by 90%" and flags its context as unverified; the endgame note says the value was "not retrieved". All three came through a summariser, so the number belongs on the in-game list.

## Status is worth what it multiplies: proc rates, tick formulas and saturation

### How a proc rolls

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Status chance is per pellet per enemy hit: `Average procs per shot = Multishot x (Forced procs + Status chance per projectile)`, times fire rate for procs per second. Above 100% "each hit may apply additional 'unique' status effects. The type of each proc is independently drawn". The type is chosen by damage share with no physical weighting since Update 27.2 (2020-03-05): 20 Impact, 5 Puncture, 10 Slash, 25 Heat and 50 Corrosive gives 18.18 / 4.55 / 9.09 / 22.73 / 45.45%. Types the enemy is status-immune to are removed and the rest renormalise ([Status Effect](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect)). **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS]** PublikDomain (forums, 2024) observed that the weighting uses the quantized distribution, so a type that quantizes to zero never procs. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Modded status chance as `base x (1 + SUM status chance mods)` is inferred; the wiki says mods "affect the base status chance" without printing a formula. A shotgun with 21 pellets at 12% lands 2.52 procs per shot and at least one proc 93.2% of the time. Base damage mods leave the weights unchanged; elemental and physical mods shift them, which is why adding elements dilutes Slash. **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer's rule of thumb from 2021-07-24: "damage types under 10 [percent] don't proc status enough to matter" ([Galvanized mods video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=imrbjrIwjxA)). Forced procs (stance hits, some weapons, Hunter Munitions) ignore status chance and distribution; the wiki flags that section as community-derived.

### The tick formula, with worked numbers

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** `Modded Base Damage = Base x (1 + base damage bonuses) x (1 + faction)`, "ignoring physical and elemental damage bonuses". Then: Slash tick `0.35 x MBD x (1 + Faction) x (1 + Status Damage) x Additional`; Heat, Toxin, Electricity and Gas tick `0.5 x MBD x (1 + that element's bonuses) x (1 + Faction) x (1 + Status Damage) x Additional`; Blast `0.3 x MBD x ...` on fuse expiry and `3 x MBD x ...` radial on early detonation. "Additional" is the crit multiplier of the hit that caused the proc and the enemy body-part multiplier, multiplied together. The wiki's examples, with base 100, Serration, a 30% Bane and Rifle Elementalist: MBD = 100 x 2.65 x 1.3 = 344.5; Slash tick = 0.35 x 344.5 x 1.3 x 1.9 = 297.82; Heat tick with Hellfire = 0.5 x 344.5 x 1.9 x 1.3 x 1.9 = 808.37 ([Slash](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Slash_Damage), [Heat](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Heat_Damage)). The pipeline note records the same rule from Damage/Calculation as `Tick = (SUM seeds + 1) x C x M`, with its example (40 x 1.55 + 1) x 0.5 x 3.25 x 1.55 = 158.68; the "+1" appears only on that page and is immaterial at real damage values.

Four consequences follow, all **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** ([Status Effect, DoT Damage Scaling](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Status_Effect#DoT_Damage_Scaling)). Single-element mods boost only their own proc; "DoTs of combined status effects (i.e. Gas, Blast) are not buffed by their component mods", so only literal Gas damage (Leaded Gas, Valence Formation) raises Gas ticks, and a Toxin mod merged into Viral buffs no tick at all. Physical mods do not buff Slash ticks. Crit tier, headshot, stealth and combo multipliers carry into the proc; Sonar weak spots do not. Each pellet rolls its own proc, so multishot scales the number of procs, not the tick size. Ticks land once per second; `Total ticks = floor(Tick rate x (Duration - Delay)) + 1`, six at base duration, so one Slash proc totals 2.1 x MBD and one unrefreshed Heat, Toxin, Electricity or Gas proc totals 3.0 x MBD x its element bonus. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED, section flagged community-derived]** A new Heat proc "both adds a stack and refreshes the duration of all currently active heat stacks", so Heat tick damage ramps without limit while refreshed, and if the first Heat proc came from a source without the weapon's Heat or faction modifiers those bonuses are lost for later procs ("Heat inherit"). **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer called Heat inherit a bug in 2021 and "a mechanic known as heat inherit" in 2024, adding that the governing first proc can belong to "even another player". **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Viral multiplies ticks at tick time, once; an attack deals its damage before applying its own Viral proc, and pellets of one shot do not benefit from each other's Viral.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Hunter Munitions gives a 30% chance of a forced Slash proc on a crit, independent of status chance, with a bleed that "only depends on the weapons modded base Damage" and scales with crit tier and headshot multipliers ([Hunter Munitions](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Hunter_Munitions)). Internal Bleeding and Hemorrhage give Impact procs a 35% chance of Slash, doubled below 2.5 fire rate; with both mods on one shot only one proc applies, at 54.5% combined (79% below 2.5) ([Internal Bleeding](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Internal_Bleeding)). Status Damage (the Elementalist mods, +90%, Update 36) is a separate bucket, "multiplicative to other damage bonuses and additive to other sources of Status Damage bonuses", sharing with Emerald shards' +30% Toxin status damage.

### Putting a number on status chance

Everything in this subsection is **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]**: the status-effects researcher's proposed model, built from confirmed inputs and validated against nothing in game. Treat each status type as an arrival stream with rate `L_t = fire rate x multishot x status chance x weight_t`. For capped statuses the steady-state stack count is min(N, cap) with N Poisson-distributed with mean L_t x duration. For Viral at 6 s:

| Viral procs per second | Mean stacks | Expected health multiplier |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.25 | 1.5 | 1.96 |
| 0.5 | 3 | 2.46 |
| 1 | 6 | 3.23 |
| 2 | 12 | 4.11 |
| 4 | 24 | 4.25 |

Corrosive at 8 s reaches 29% expected strip at 0.25 procs per second, 44% at 0.5, 65% at 1 and 80% at 2. DoT damage per second is `L_t x ticks x tick` for Slash, Toxin, Electricity and Gas, and `L_heat x T x tick / 2` averaged over a window T for refreshing Heat. The researcher's worked weapon (base 100 split 30/30/40, +180% Viral, +90% Heat, fire rate 6, multishot 1.9, status 78%) lands 8.89 procs per second: Viral 4.33, Heat 2.16, Slash 0.96. Viral saturates at 4.25; Slash ticks of 0.35 x 265 = 92.75 give 535 DPS before Viral and 2,273 on health with it; Heat reaches about 21.6 stacks after 10 s for 5,445 per second before armour and Viral. The value of a status chance mod is the change in modelled DPS it causes, and the table shows why it varies so much: raising Viral from 2 to 4 procs per second is worth +3%, from 0.25 to 0.5 is worth +26%, while DoT damage is linear in proc rate and never saturates. The model ignores burst structure and the rule that a hit does not benefit from its own proc, so slow, high-multishot weapons need a per-shot simulation. **[CREATOR-STATED]** TheKengineer's "first shot effect" is the same objection from the player's side: "before those status procs are applied your initial shot will be dealing less damage; the average dps up to any given point is lower than your current bonus", and "if you are killing things in one or two shots already there's not enough time to land the status procs" (2021-07-24).

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Target profiles cut status value hard. Fully status-immune: Eidolons, Exploiter and Profit-Taker Orbs, Ropalolyst, Tusk Thumpers, Arbitration drones (list flagged incomplete). Viral-proc immune: Ambulas, several Deimos units, Infested Demolishers, Techrot Babau. Acolytes take at most 4 stacks of any status (Impact 3) and are neutral to every element since Update 36; **[WIKI-VIA-SUMMARY]** Liches and Void Angels cap at 4 (Impact 6). At four stacks Viral is x2.75 not x4.25, and Corrosive strips 44% for 3.27x not 5.98x. Against Overguard only Magnetic amplifies. **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS, old Fandom page]** Necramechs cap at 4 stacks; treat as unverified.

On element choice the mechanics support Viral plus Slash, Viral plus Heat, and Corrosive plus Heat on armour, and Magnetic against Corpus and Overguard; but the community evidence the status researcher found for "what players use in 2026" is one unauthored guide site "current through Update 43.5" ([warframe.today](https://warframe.today/best-status-weapons/)) and search snippets. The researcher's lists of "still correct" and "outdated" advice are **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** judged against the formulas. Advice that is certainly outdated because a patch note removed its basis: Corrosive permanently stripping armour, Viral halving health, Slash bypassing shields, physical procs weighted 4x and "100% status shotguns" (all pre-Update 27.2); Blast as an accuracy debuff (pre-Update 36); Toxin or Heat mods scaling Gas (pre-Update 27.2); Electricity procs ticking separately (pre-Update 33.6).

## The modding systems: exclusivity, stacks, capacity, arcanes, Rivens and special weapons

### Exclusivity follows hidden inheritance, and a machine-readable list exists

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** "Players cannot install duplicate mods onto a single piece of equipment. Likewise, different variants of the same mod cannot be equipped together"; the variants are Normal, Flawed, Primed, Umbra, Amalgam, Galvanized and Archon ([Mod](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod)). The actual rule is hidden inheritance: "Two mods with the same hidden parent type or has a parent/child relationship cannot both be equipped", and name similarity is not the rule ([Mod/Compatibility](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod/Compatibility)). Non-obvious wiki-stated pairs: corrupted crit-chance mods conflict with their plain analogues (Critical Delay with Point Strike, Critical Deceleration with Blunderbuss, Creeping Bullseye with Pistol Gambit); Split Flights conflicts with Split Chamber; Mending Shot conflicts with Thunderbolt; Galvanized mods "cannot be equipped with their other counterparts" (Chamber with Split Chamber, Hell with Hell's Chamber, Diffusion with Barrel Diffusion, Aptitude with Rifle Aptitude, Savvy with Shotgun Savvy, Shot with Sure Shot, Scope with Argon Scope, Crosshairs with Hydraulic Crosshairs, Acceleration with Fatal Acceleration) ([Galvanized Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Galvanized_Mods)). ### Capacity, drain and polarity

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Guns have eight general slots plus an Exilus slot (needs an adapter) plus one arcane slot (needs an adapter); capacity equals rank, normally 30 and 40 on some weapons, doubled by an Orokin Catalyst; "matching polarity reduces drain by half, rounded up (e.g. Serration costs 14, but dropped into a Madurai polarized slot costs only 7)" ([Mod](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Mod), [Exilus Weapon Adapter](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Exilus_Weapon_Adapter)). **[DATA-CONFIRMED]** Drain at rank r is `baseDrain + r` (Serration 4 + 10 = 14; a Riven 10 + 8 = 18). Exilus mods consume capacity, exilus-eligible gun mods are those flagged `isUtility`, and none adds damage directly. Arcanes use no capacity, one per gun, maximum rank 5 ([Arcane Enhancement](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Arcane_Enhancement)). **[DISPUTED between two wiki pages]** The Mod page rounds the wrong-polarity penalty "mathematically" with an explicit table (drain 2 to 5 adds 1, 6 to 9 adds 2, 10 to 13 adds 3, 14 to 16 adds 4); the Polarity page says "increase its cost by 25%, rounded up". Only round-half-up fits the table, so the modding researcher uses `floor(1.25 x drain + 0.5)`.

```
capacity   = rank x (2 if catalyst else 1)          30 -> 30/60 ; 40 -> 40/80
drain      = baseDrain + modRank
slot cost  = ceil(drain / 2)              matching polarity (or Universal slot, non-Umbra mod)
           = floor(drain x 1.25 + 0.5)    different polarity   (rounding DISPUTED)
           = drain                        unpolarized
legal iff SUM(slot cost over 8 slots + exilus) <= capacity
```

The worked build (Galvanized Chamber 16, Galvanized Aptitude 12, Critical Delay 9, Vital Sense 9, Primed Cryo Rounds 16, Infected Clip 11, Primed Shred 16, Hunter Munitions 9, Primed Stabilizer 12 in the Exilus) costs 110 unpolarized against a capacity of 60: illegal with zero Forma. Fully matched it costs 57: legal. One mismatched slot for Primed Cryo Rounds costs 20 and breaks it at 69; swapping a max Riven in for Hunter Munitions gives 61, illegal by one point, "which is why rank-40 weapons (80 capacity) are far easier to fit". **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Kuva, Tenet and Coda weapons gain two ranks per polarization to rank 40 after five Forma; **[DATA-CONFIRMED]** `maxLevelCap: 40` is present on 52 weapons and should be read, not inferred from the name. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** Slot assignment is a small assignment problem (nine mods, nine slots), and under-ranking a Primed mod is a legitimate lever when a build misses by one or two points.

### Rivens

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** A Riven has two or three positives and at most one negative; each value is the base value times the weapon's disposition times a layout multiplier times a roll between 0.9 and 1.1, scaled linearly with rank. Disposition runs from 0.5 to 1.55 and is re-balanced quarterly ([Riven Mods](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Riven_Mods)). **[COMMUNITY, code that reproduces the wiki table]** `value = tagValue x 1.5 x disposition x 10 x lerp(0.9, 1.1, Value / 0x3FFFFFFF) x numBuffsAtten[buffCount] x 1.25^curseCount x (lvl + 1)` ([RivenParser.js](https://github.com/calamity-inc/warframe-riven-info/blob/senpai/RivenParser.js)). ## Melee is a different calculator: attack type and combo state come before mods

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Melee shares the base, elemental, crit and faction layers with guns but has no multishot, adds a per-swing stance multiplier, and applies the combo multiplier only to heavy attacks: "Melee Combo Multiplier does not multiply the damage of your normal attacks" ([Melee Combo](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee_Combo), [Damage/Calculation](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Damage/Calculation)). Condition Overload (+80% per status type, uncapped) adds into the Pressure Point bucket: `Total = Base x [1 + Damage Mods + 0.8 x n] x (1 + Elemental Mods)`; "this damage does not apply to Slams, Heavy Slams, or Radial Attack explosions" ([Condition Overload](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Condition_Overload)). Killing Blow's +120% on heavy attacks is additive with Pressure Point; Seismic Wave's slam bonus is multiplicative. A heavy attack is `modded normal damage x class heavy multiplier x combo multiplier`; the wiki's example is a Staff at 400 x 5 x 3 = 6,000. Worked from the melee note: 120 base with Primed Pressure Point (+165%), Condition Overload at three statuses (+240%) and +120% Viral on a 200% stance swing is 120 x 5.05 = 606, x 2.2 = 1,333.2, x 2.0 = 2,666.4 before crit; a Heavy Blade (6x) with 250 base, Primed Pressure Point and Killing Blow at 12x combo is 250 x 3.85 x 6 x 12 = 69,300. Because Primed Pressure Point and Condition Overload share a bucket, adding the first to a five-status Condition Overload build is worth 1,330 / 1,000 = +33%. The combined order of operations in the melee note is **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** assembled from separately confirmed formulas.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** The combo counter gains one point per 100% of stance multiplier per enemy hit, steps one tier per 20 points, caps at 12x at 220 points, and clears after 5 s (Venka Prime 13x; Xoris infinite duration). Blood Rush and Weeping Wounds each add 40% of base crit or status chance per tier above the first: `Crit Chance = Weapon CC x [1 + mods + 0.4 x (Combo Multi - 1)] + flat`; the Gladiator set adds 10% per set mod per tier into the same term ([Blood Rush](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Blood_Rush), [Weeping Wounds](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Weeping_Wounds)). True Steel (+120%), Sacrificial Steel (+220%) and Galvanized Steel (+110%) double on heavy attacks. A 25% crit weapon with Sacrificial Steel, Blood Rush and three Gladiator mods at 12x has 25% x (1 + 2.2 + 0.7 x 11) = 272.5% crit chance. Heavy Attack Efficiency keeps a share of combo, capped at 90%; initial combo regenerates at 40 points per second. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED, page flagged as work in progress]** Class heavy multipliers run from 2x (Glaive) through 5x or 6x for most classes to 12x and 15x (Sparring); follow-through decays damage geometrically per extra enemy (0.4 for Hammers to 0.9 for Daggers, 1.0 for Exalted weapons) and does not apply to slams or projectiles ([Melee](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee)). Slams since Update 35.5 (2024-03-27) are pure radial damage at 2x a normal attack (3x for heavy slams, times combo) with falloff to 50% (70%); Update 39 (2025-06-25) tied slam radius to drop height. **[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Tennokai (Update 35) gives each melee hit a 15% chance, or every fourth hit with Discipline's Merit, to open a 2 s window in which a heavy attack costs no combo; Truth's Flame (Update 42, 2026-03-25) adds +120% Tennokai damage and a chain on kill. **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]** With a 1,000-damage modded hit on a Heavy Blade at 12x, four light swings at 300% deal 12,000 and the free heavy deals 72,000, so a calculator that ignores Tennokai understates hybrid builds several-fold.

**[WIKI-CONFIRMED]** Thirteen melee arcanes exist. Melee Influence (on Electricity status, 20% chance for an 18 s window in which elemental statuses spread to enemies within 20 m, with faction damage applied twice to the spread damage and three times to its procs) does not spread Slash, Impact or Puncture; Melee Duplicate repeats tier-1 crits, with optimum crit chance `(3 x CM - 4) / (2 x CM - 2)` ([Melee Influence](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee_Influence), [Melee Duplicate](https://wiki.warframe.com/w/Melee_Duplicate)). **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS, one forum thread, May 2026]** Influence is "easily put in a tier above the rest" and Afflictions has "quite insane scaling on a handful of melee weapons" ([forum thread](https://forums.warframe.com/topic/1506886-suggestions-on-buffing-melee-arcanes-to-bring-them-to-the-tier-of-influence-and-afflictions/)). **[COMMUNITY-CONSENSUS, wiki page marked unofficial]** Condition Overload's bonus varies by stance input (Gnashing Payara neutral 36% of the listed bonus, Mountain's Edge heavy 233%), and some weapon projectiles take it multiplicatively.

The melee note is the weakest on "what experts do". No Brozime, TheKengineer or Tactical Potato melee source was read; the stance comparison table comes from a wiki page flagged as omitting stance physical bonuses; the six templates (crit light attack, hybrid, heavy, slam, Influence, glaive) are "constructed from confirmed mod effects; they are not copied from a creator". ### The decision process and its thresholds

**[CREATOR-STATED]** Brozime, 2023-12-09: "the big important thing about weapon modding is you want to increase your damage in diverse ways... by doing either multi-shot, damage, elementals, and crits", and "if two things are multiplicative, you want about even amounts of them to go up at the same time. And if things are additive, you would rather diversify unless you're getting extremely large numbers" ([Basic Modding YOU SHOULD KNOW](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FiSbyWvaHbE)). TheKengineer, 2021-08-01: doubling each of five multiplied terms gives 32x, while raising elemental damage by 100% five times gives 6x. He assigns a weapon one of four roles before modding, "direct damage, status damage, converted slash damage, and then weapon primers" (2022-02-02). The stated thresholds are few. On crit: Brozime, "Weapons with like 5% crit, you're not going to add these cuz it's just too inconsistent... 25% crit chance, usually very worth it"; TheKengineer, "if you got a crit of 20 percent or above work with the crits" (2020-05-11), with the rider that a low-crit weapon stacking a flat source such as Arcane Avenger is "effectively, a critical-based weapon - just with extra steps" (2021-06-19). Nobody read gives a numeric threshold for status chance; TheKengineer replaces the question with expected procs per kill.

His Galvanized status rule is the most explicit algorithm in the notes. **[CREATOR-STATED, 2021-07-24, pre-Update-36 and derived while a projectile bug existed]** With a separate primer: "if you can proc a minimum of two different status effects Galvanized Shot is better than Hornet Strike on pistols... with three different status procs all three status overload mods are the single best pure damage mod". Self-priming: the weapon needs "three or more possible status types and at least three of those are each 10 [percent] or more of your total damage", counting forced procs; estimate shots to kill; procs per kill = maximum multishot x status chance x shots to kill; "eight or more procs per kill for primaries, six or more for pistols, and the status overload is a better choice" than the base damage mod. His toy example shows why the displayed figure misleads: a CO mod shows 340% but averages 220% over two shots, losing to Primed Point Blank's flat 265%; and with five status types "even after 12 different status procs are applied there's a less than one in four chance of all five status types being represented" ([Galvanized mods video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=imrbjrIwjxA)). **[UNKNOWN]** Whether the 8 and 6 thresholds survive current mod values; no post-Update-36 restatement was found.

On Slash conversion: **[CREATOR-STATED]** "For secondaries and primaries alike, if the weapon doesn't have a forced impact proc on its main damage, [Internal Bleeding and Hemorrhage] simply aren't worth it"; "Hunter Munitions is far more reliable... Due to it scaling off crits instead of status"; "any buff to your fire rate which brings you over the 2.5 threshold will halve your slash output per shot... watch out for buffs like Wisp's haste mote" (2021-06-19, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yy_vzLI1h9Y)). Post-Update-36 he adds that a Slash proc out-damages the direct hit only "if the target has any more than 65% damage resistance" (2024-06-26). On physical mods: "there is precious little reason to use the physical damage mods"; "puncture shouldn't be modded for, merely enjoyed when it exists". On multishot: "Generally, multi-shot is great for every gun". On crit damage mods being worth more than they read: going from 3x to 6x "increases the bonus on a yellow crit from two times to five times extra damage".

On outside buffs: **[CREATOR-STATED, 2024-05-01]** "Roar should be your default option for damage increasing as a result of being both reliable and effective... You never need to question if your weapon is using additive CO or not." Eclipse multiplies pure damage, but on weapons with additive CO "that whole CO bonus also ignores Eclipse as if it were a pure damage bonus", so "Eclipse has anti-synergy with status priming"; it wins only "if you do have non-additive CO and you're relying on direct damage, not status damage, and you already have a faction bonus applied from something else" ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WUMPxnpmtKQ)). That video predates Update 36 by seven weeks; the Roar mechanics match the current wiki. The pipeline note gives full-strength Eclipse as +200% (x3); the expert note says subsumed Eclipse is "only a 30% base increase"; both describe the same ability at different strengths, and the expert researcher lists Eclipse's current numbers as not verified. The derived effects of each buff on mod choice (Roar present devalues Bane from x1.55 to a relative x1.42 at 30% Roar; Nourish frees the Viral slots; a reliable full strip removes the case for Corrosive, Hunter Munitions and Heat's armour value) are **[RESEARCHER-INFERENCE]**.

On handling: experts treat it as a set of vetoes, not a score. **[CREATOR-STATED]** "Increased recoil can make some guns hard to control, while reduced magazine can see you reloading far too often"; "range is simultaneously crucial and forgettable, as it's mostly a case of having enough range to be effective"; "weapons with high damage attacks using a few shots may find attack speed just less valuable, especially if you're one-shotting enemies" (TheKengineer, 2021 to 2022). ### Where the experts disagree

The crit threshold differs (20% for TheKengineer in 2020, about 25% for Brozime in 2023) and neither addresses 10 to 20%. Default elements differ in level of optimisation: Brozime says Viral then Heat "on everything" for new players (pre-Update-36) and Heat or Magnetic as a weapon's fixed bonus element (2025 to 2026); TheKengineer gives a per-faction map with status effect as the tie-breaker (2024). **[DISPUTED]** The value of Slash: Brozime in 2023 calls Slash procs "one of the best procs in the game" and Hunter Munitions "one of the best mods"; TheKengineer in 2024 says Slash-reliant builds "have become weaker" and in 2021 that "slash is actually the weakest for raw damage out of all status effects. What makes it desirable at all is that it skips armor entirely". This is probably a before-and-after-Update-36 difference, but Brozime's current view was not obtained. Faction mods are valued highly on mechanics (and Brozime published "We Use Bane Mods Now" on 2021-07-27, title only), while TheKengineer treats faction-locked stats as a late, loadout-specific optimisation. Brozime refuses to write modding rules down; TheKengineer reduces them to formulas. And TheKengineer disagrees with his earlier self on Heat inherit (bug in 2021, mechanic in 2024) and on Radiation and Corrosive (his 2020 advice reversed in 2024: "radiation no longer has an up to seven times damage bonus against alloy armored units"), which the researcher uses to argue for preferring the newest dated statement.

## Conclusion

The research changes what the advisor's unit of output should be. A recommendation is not a build with a DPS figure; it is a build, a target, a set of stated assumptions and two or three numbers, because the same eight mods are worth a 42-fold different amount depending on whether the target's armour is stripped and whether Viral is up, and because the experts the owner trusts evaluate marginal gain per bucket and then apply vetoes. The reassuring finding is that the calculator's arithmetic is not the problem: nobody the researchers could read says Overframe's numbers are computed wrongly, the app already matches the arsenal on three guns, and the stacking rules have been stable and wiki-confirmed for years. What is missing is everything after the arsenal number, and almost all of it has a formula with a wiki citation and a data source already on disk.

Three things should temper confidence. The creator evidence is one creator deep: without TheKengineer's transcripts there would be almost no explicit expert rule in this report, his most relevant videos predate Update 36 or were unreadable, and Tactical Potato is absent. The game moved under the research: Archimedea gained Arsenal Boosts of unknown value on 2026-09-23 and Rivens gain new stat types on 2026-10-07, so both areas need re-reading before code is written against them. And the largest single uncertainty for boss content, the post-Update-40 attenuation ratios, is something Digital Extremes has chosen not to publish. The practical response is the one the expert-heuristics researcher proposes and the endgame researcher independently repeats: stamp every rule with the update it was last verified against, and let the player see and switch every assumption.

## 1. What the advisor should model next, ranked by impact on Steel Path and Elite Archimedea recommendations

The ranking is this report's **[SYNTHESIS]** of the five researchers' separate "must model" lists, ordered by how far each item can move the order of recommended builds for hard content. The size estimates are the researchers' computed figures cited in the sections above.

1. **A target, with armour.** Add an enemy panel (Grineer Heavy Gunner, Corrupted Heavy Gunner, Corpus Tech, an Eximus, Scaldra Eradicator, Techrot Scaart, a Murmur unit) at a chosen level, with `DR = 0.9 x sqrt(armour / 2700)`, the 2,700 cap, the faction x1.5 / x0.5 table (not applied to Overguard), and the mode multiplier separate from level (Steel Path x2.5; Archimedea, Netracells and Archon Hunts x2.0 or x2.5 solo to x4.0 by squad). Armour state alone is worth up to 10x; without a target, faction mods, Corrosive, Heat, Viral, Magnetic and Slash all score zero, as they do today.
2. **Conditional stacks and the arcane slot, with an opening number and a sustained number.** Galvanized multishot at +230%, the +360% to +480% damage arcanes in Serration's bucket, Frostbite, Blight and Voltage multishot and crit damage. This is the item that decides whether Serration or Hornet Strike belongs in a build at all (+165% without the arcane, +36% with it), and it is the handoff's own first-ranked gap. Default kill-triggered stacks to full for mission play and to zero for a boss scenario; show both.
3. **The status layer.** Proc rate per type (`fire rate x multishot x status chance x damage share`), expected Viral and Magnetic multipliers and Corrosive and Heat strip with stack caps, the tick formulas with faction squared and the single-element rule, Heat's refresh, Hunter Munitions, and the Elementalist status-damage bucket. Viral alone is up to 4.25x; faction on ticks is 2.4x for a Primed Bane. Include boss stack caps (4) and immunity flags.
4. **Capacity and polarity legality from the inventory.** Rank, catalyst, Forma'd polarities, drain and the slot-assignment problem. For Archimedea this outranks its usual position: offered weapons are often unranked or un-Forma'd, and a recommendation that does not fit is useless. Report "Forma needed" when the best build does not fit.
5. **Weak points and head-crit rules by faction, behind an aim toggle.** `(Body + k x weak point bonuses) x (1 + headshot bonuses)`, 3x heads with crit doubling for Grineer, Corrupted, Scaldra and Infested, 3x without doubling for Corpus, about 1.5x for Murmur and Techrot. Up to 6x for crit builds, and the difference between a Steel Path build and an Elite Deep Archimedea build.
6. **Per-attack data: direct versus radial, the gun Condition Overload flag, and trigger types.** Read `behaviours` and the wiki or WFCD `attacks`; score CO and weak-point mods against the direct portion only; add the per-weapon CO behaviour flag (adding, multiplying, none, partial); implement the charge, burst, bow, beam and duplex fire-rate formulas. This unlocks the 30 of 85 guns the advisor currently refuses and stops launchers scoring low.
7. **Archimedea week inputs and the offered-loadout mode.** Three offered weapons per slot, the Deviation, Risk Variables and Personal Modifiers, and the damage switches that matter (Sealed Armor x0.1 off weak points, Heavy Warfare x0.05 for non-heavy weapons, Elemental Potency, Bolstered Belligerents, Low Yield, the ammo modifiers, Dull Blades). Output the best of the offered weapons from owned mods at real capacity, the shortfall against a free choice, and whether dropping the row (3 points, with the Legendary pool at 34 of 37) is the better trade. This is the use case a build site cannot serve.
8. **Kuva, Tenet and Coda bonus elements and Rivens.** Both are already in the inventory in decodable form, and the Riven decoder exists in the app. Lich weapons are understated by 25 to 60% of base today, and a Riven is frequently the strongest mod a player owns.
9. **Exclusivity from the wiki's `Incompatible` lists, plus the stat locks.** Replace the name-stem heuristic; model "Multishot cannot be modified" (Acuity) as the app already models the Cannonade fire-rate lock; add class- and variant-restricted mods and augments through `compat` and `compatibilityTags`.
10. **External buffs as toggles.** Roar (faction bucket), Vex Armor-style buffs (base bucket), Eclipse (final multiplier that additive CO ignores), Nourish and Smite Infusion (extra element), Xata's Whisper (separate instance), a primer's status list, armour strip from abilities, and Archon Shards. Each changes which mods are worth a slot.
11. **Melee.** A separate calculator with attack type, combo state, stance data, heavy multipliers, Tennokai and melee arcanes. It ranks this low only because it is a large build on top of nothing; for Archimedea, where melee is exempt from every ammo modifier and fills one of the four loadout rows, it is the biggest missing weapon class.
12. **Incarnon forms and evolution perks.** Score against the form's stats with the selected perks; needs a hand-maintained perk table, since no structured source has one.
13. **Boss attenuation as a cap.** For enemies on the post-40.0.2 list, soft-cap per-hit and per-second damage in proportion to max health and report "reaches cap" instead of DPS; treat Demolishers, Necramechs, Babau and Dedicants as plain health pools. Ratios are unknown for most bosses, so this is a shape, not a number.
14. **Ammo economy and handling flags.** Time until dry, reload interval, effective range, "needs weak-point kills", "only works under 2.5 fire rate", contradictory mod pairs. These are the experts' veto conditions and the checks that would catch the Overframe failure modes.
15. **Explanations and date stamps.** A reason per slot in bucket terms, the marginal gain of each mod and of the best unowned mod, and the update each rule was verified against.
16. **Arch-guns**, for Heavy Warfare weeks and Profit-Taker.
17. **Search quality.** Replace the greedy single-swap search once conditional buckets exist, because bucket interactions (arcane against Serration, CO against base damage, Viral against DoT mods) create the local optima a greedy search gets stuck in.
18. **Quantization, crit-multiplier encoding, the Vigilante set, sniper combo, beam ramp-up.** Needed only to match Simulacrum numbers or for narrow weapon classes; under 3% in most cases.

## 2. Rules the app currently gets wrong or omits

The app's behaviour is taken from `HANDOFF.md` and `damage-rules.md` (both dated 2026-10-05); the corrections come from the notes as cited in the sections above. Items marked "wrong" contradict the app's own documentation; items marked "omits" are acknowledged gaps that the notes now quantify or supply data for.

1. **Wrong: "Radial damage is missing from the weapon data."** It is in the bundled PEP under `behaviours[].projectile.explosiveAttack` and in the bundled WFCD under `attacks[]` with falloff. Trumna Prime's primary-fire radial (50 Heat) is in WFCD and the wiki but not PEP.
2. **Wrong: set bonuses are "not listed" in `@wfcd/items`.** They are separate `Mod Set Mod` entries in `Mods.json`, and structured in PEP `ExportModSet.json`.
3. **Wrong in emphasis: quantization stated as 1/32.** The quantum is disputed (1/32 on the current wiki, 1/16 in 2024 community tests), and DoT ticks are not quantized at all.
4. **Wrong for some weapons: reading `damagePerShot`.** PEP's README says `behaviours` should be preferred, and on projectile weapons the `impact` block is a placeholder with the real numbers under `projectile`. The data researcher lists "projectile weapons reading wrong damage" as a gap; no specific failing weapon was demonstrated.
5. **Wrong ranking signal: burst DPS alone.** With no arcane, Serration and Hornet Strike are scored at full face value; under a full-stack +360% arcane Serration is worth +36%, not +165%.
6. **Wrong by omission: Galvanized mods counted at their unconditional value**, so the plain form outranks them. Experts assume kill stacks are up in mission play (TheKengineer, 2021-07-24).
7. **Fragile: the mod-family heuristic.** Families matched by shared path stem or name prefix cannot be relied on for the renamed Galvanized pairs, the corrupted crit pairs (Critical Delay with Point Strike, Critical Deceleration with Blunderbuss, Creeping Bullseye with Pistol Gambit), Split Flights, Mending Shot with Thunderbolt, or Serration with Higasa and Spectral Serration. The one-inventory sweep that found no bad pair does not cover mods the search did not pick.
8. **Omits: any mod with a rule other than the fire-rate lock is never recommended.** That excludes the Acuity mods (multishot lock), which define the weak-point archetype.
9. **Omits: mods for narrower weapon classes and variant-specific augments**, although PEP's `compat` and `compatibilityTags` link them.
10. **Omits: the enemy.** No faction modifiers, armour, shields, Overguard, Viral or damage-over-time, so faction, status and armour-related mods add nothing.
11. **Omits: weak points and head-crit doubling**, which are faction-dependent by a factor of four.
12. **Omits: the arcane, including its passive.** The Trumna Prime reload mismatch (3.1 s shown against 4.00 s computed) is explained by Primary Merciless's "+30% Reload Speed" at rank 5, which the rules page records as "from memory and unchecked"; it is in the Public Export text (4.00 / 1.3 = 3.08).
13. **Omits: capacity and polarity**, although the inventory holds rank, catalyst and Forma'd polarities.
14. **Omits: Kuva, Tenet and Coda bonus elements**, which are on the weapon in the inventory as `UpgradeFingerprint.buffs`.
15. **Omits: Rivens**, reported as unrecognised although `decodeAllRivens()` already returns their stats by tag. Riven elements also combine bottom-up, which the slot-order rule does not cover.
16. **Omits: charge, burst, bow, held and duplex weapons**, for which the wiki gives closed-form effective fire rates and PEP gives burst counts and delays. The semi-auto cap of 10 shots per second is not mentioned in the rules page.
17. **Omits: beam behaviour** (merged multishot ticks, 0.5 ammo per tick, ramp-up) once held weapons are admitted.
18. **Omits: three new weapons** that DE's live export and the bundled WFCD already carry, because `KEPT_FIELDS` in `services/publicExportSource.ts` discards stat fields; and Kitguns, which need assembling from `ModularParts`.
19. **Omits: Incarnon forms and perks, set bonuses (Vigilante), Archon Shards, and external buffs.**
20. **Unverified in the rules page but now sourced:** reload `base / (1 + bonus)` and summed bonuses are WIKI-CONFIRMED; "innate element combines last" is WIKI-CONFIRMED; the arsenal's per-pellet status is WIKI-CONFIRMED. Magazine rounding remains UNKNOWN in every source.
21. **Search: greedy with single swaps.** Acknowledged in the handoff; it matters more once buckets interact.
22. **Presentation: a single number with no stated target, assumptions or date**, which is the pattern the Overframe criticism is aimed at.

## 3. Questions that can only be settled in game

Consolidated from the eight notes' open-question lists and de-duplicated. Each names the cheapest test where a researcher proposed one.

Damage pipeline
