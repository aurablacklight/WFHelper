## Changes in beta.6

- Corufell Prime now counts toward mastery and shows under Archwing. It was missing from the mastery list.
- A finished update download stays ready to install. Before, the startup update check could reset it and bring back the Download button.
- A copy of the app that was never installed, such as a shortcut pinned to a build folder, no longer offers updates it cannot apply. It asks you to open the installed app instead.
